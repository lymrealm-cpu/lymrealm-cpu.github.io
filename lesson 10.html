LESSON 10

NETWORK PORTS & SERVICES

1. What is a Network Port?

A network port is a communication endpoint used by computers and servers to provide different network services.

Different services commonly use different port numbers.

2. Common Network Ports

PortService
21FTP
22SSH
53DNS
80HTTP
443HTTPS

3. Practical Training

Check your CURL connection:

curl -v https://example.com

Look for the connection information. HTTPS normally uses port 443.

4. Important Information

  • Port 80 is commonly used by HTTP.
  • Port 443 is commonly used by HTTPS.
  • Port 22 is commonly used by SSH.
  • Only test systems you own or have permission to test.

5. Knowledge Test

Which port is commonly used by HTTPS?

A) 80

B) 443

C) 22

Correct Answer: B) 443
