Lesson 01: Introduction to Cyber Security

What is Cyber Security?

Cyber Security means protecting computers, mobile devices, networks and websites from digital attacks and unauthorized access.

What is Ethical Hacking?

Ethical hacking means testing computer systems with proper permission to find security weaknesses and help fix them.

Types of Hackers

White Hat: Works with permission to improve security.

Black Hat: Uses hacking for illegal activities.

Grey Hat: May test systems without permission.

Three Golden Rules

1. Always get permission before testing.

2. Never steal or damage data.

3. Report security problems responsibly.
